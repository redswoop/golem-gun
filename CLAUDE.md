# Golem Gun

A Minecraft Bedrock add-on: material wands repaint a mob, and the Golem Rod turns a mob into an iron golem wearing that mob's head. Shared Bedrock notes are in `../CLAUDE.md`.

Keep kids' names and personal details out of this folder, including test player names.

## Commands

- `npm test`: runs the real pack scripts against the fake game in `test/mock`, which checks properties and spawn events against the generated entity files. It proves logic only, not looks or visibility.
- `tools/install.sh`: installs into the Mac dev packs and pins every world that uses the pack. It never closes the game: if the game is running it waits for Armen to quit, so run it in the background. Armen withdrew permission to close his game for installs.
- `./build.sh`: runs `tools/check_pack.py` (versions, and every texture, model, controller and UV a mob uses), then writes `GolemGun.mcaddon`.
- `python3 tools/make_pack.py "<game version dir>/assets/assets"`: regenerates everything except the manifests and the hand-written scripts (`main.js`, `convert.js`, `drops.js`, `twists.js`, `breeding.js`, `material.js`, `debug.js`), including recipes, the golem loot table and `scripts/data.js`. It needs Pillow.

## How it works

- The design is the farm-tech plan in `docs/farm-tech-plan.md` ("mobs are the mines"). Phase 1 (wand ladder, one-time mutation, sterile painted mobs, debug tools) is in 0.4.0; production, mob jobs, boosts and the fire chicken are still to come.
- A vanilla mob's texture can't be changed by script, so a wand swaps the mob for a lookalike (`golemgun:cow`, `golemgun:iron_golem`) whose material property picks the texture. Since 0.8.0 that's `golemgun:mat`, an int index into `MATERIALS`; the `golemgun:material` enum holds only `LEGACY` (the first 15) and is what mobs painted before 0.8.0 have, with `mat` at -1. `material.js` (`materialOf`, `setMaterialOf`) reads `mat` first and falls back to the enum, and still writes the enum for legacy materials; the client Molang does the same (`material_index()`), so old worlds need no migration. Never reorder `MATERIALS`, and the first 15 must stay `LEGACY`.
- A mutation happens once (Armen's call): wands only work on vanilla mobs, and refuse painted mobs, painted golems and head golems. `/scriptevent golem:set_element` gets round it for testing.
- Painted farm animals breed (0.8.0; sterile before, which was the economy's brake until Armen's son wanted to put a stone and a wood sheep together). `make_mob` keeps `minecraft:breedable` for mobs with a baby form that don't pop, pointed at their own painted kind, and strips it from monsters; egg laying (`spawn_entity`) is still stripped. The game spawns the baby and `breeding.js` gives it its material on `entitySpawn` with cause `Born`: the two nearest grown mobs of the kind are the parents; a listed pair (`hybrid` in `MATERIALS`, `HYBRIDS` in data.js) makes a hybrid, otherwise one parent's material at random, so same-material pairs breed true. Hybrids (clay, aquamarine, steel, amethyst, cake, moss, boulder, pygmy, ember, gum) have no wand, breed true, and get no head golem sheets: the Golem Rod on a hybrid mob makes a plain iron golem with the mob's own head (the client falls back to index 0 for `mat` >= 15). Bred babies grow up normally; the 4 babies a kill leaves ("shards") still never grow up, or a kill would be a free exponential farm.
- The swap keeps name, health, age, climate, and what a mob wears (`worn` in `MOBS`: saddle, shorn wool, a creeper's charge). Held items and armor are lost. Mobs with an owner are refused, because a swap would lose the owner. The rod drops a pig's saddle as an item.
- Painted mobs never despawn and never transform (zombie to drowned, skeleton to stray, pig to zombified piglin): `make_mob` strips `minecraft:despawn` and `minecraft:transformation` from every component group.
- Drops (`drops.js`), on top of vanilla loot: a painted farm animal with a baby form (cows, chickens, sheep, pigs, foxes, wolves) splits into 4 babies of its material when it dies, and drops no material itself. Monsters with a baby form (zombies, husks, drowned, piglins, zombified piglins; `pops` in `MOBS`) pop instead, flinging what 4 babies would have dropped, because in 0.5.0 four baby zombies fought back (Armen's call, 0.6.0). The farm babies ("shards") never grow up and never split again: `add_shard` generates a `golemgun:as_shard` event with the baby group minus `minecraft:ageable` (monster babies never age anyway). A baby drops 1 of its material, and a mob with no baby form drops 1-2. Either way each `rare` drop rolls its own chance: 10% for the next tier's material by default, and stone also has 20% coal. Golems have a poppy-only loot table, so their iron comes from the script too: raw iron for an unpainted head golem. A painted creeper's blast breaks blocks as usual, and then every block lining the hole turns into its element (`CRATERS` in `make_pack.py`): iron ore for an iron creeper, with 1-2 blocks of the next tier up (gold ore) hidden in it, a bounce pit of slime (Armen, 0.7.0: all of it the element, plus one or two of the next tier). The holes come from `getImpactedBlocks()` in `world.beforeEvents.explosion` (an exploding creeper isn't a death), and the lining is their neighbours that aren't holes. Builds turn too (Armen: everything, not just natural blocks), but never bedrock, portals, spawners, anything with an inventory, or two-part and not-really-a-block things like beds, doors, plants and torches (`KEEP` and `NOT_A_WALL`). A blast that breaks nothing (mobGriefing off, or in mid-air) throws 3-5 `burst` ore blocks instead. `check_pacing()` keeps diamond ore and ancient debris out of craters below gold and diamond, and `check_blocks()` checks every crater block id against the game.
- Wands are crafted. A new player gets one Wood Wand (once, tracked by a player dynamic property). The ladder is `LADDER` (from `tier` in `MATERIALS`): wood, stone, iron, gold, diamond, netherite. Coal came off it in 0.7.0 (Armen: "coal to get diamonds is dumb, because you can just mine some coal"), so diamonds first turn up as gold mobs' rare drop. Each wand is the tier below's wand with 4 of its `craft` item around it (8 for diamond, the whole ring), which uses that wand up. Wands off the ladder start from the ladder wand of their depth (`from`): copper, slime, TNT and honey from wood, lapis and emerald from iron, quartz and coal from gold, obsidian from diamond (Armen: always the previous tier's wand; obsidian was lying around at spawn). Their mobs still drop (and will produce) their material: lapis mobs drop lapis. The rod is an iron golem shape (iron blocks, carved pumpkin) with a Wood Wand in the middle. `/scriptevent golem:give` (cheats on) still hands over everything.
- Extra drops: each material's `also` lists things found where it's found, and each mob's `habitat` lists things from where it lives (drowned bring up kelp). `check_pacing()` stops the build if anything but gold, diamond or netherite mobs would hand out diamonds or netherite.
- Forms (`form` in `MATERIALS`, `FORM_GROUPS`): a component group every painted entity has, added by event (`applyForm` in `material.js`) after painting, when a bred baby is born (fragile only) and when it grows up (`dataDrivenEntityTrigger` for `minecraft:ageable_grow_up`). Wood is `fragile` (health capped at 4 whatever the mob; Armen: "it takes ages to kill one" by punching), boulder `giant` (`minecraft:scale` 2, health 40, slow; the same component babies use at 0.5, since resizing golems by model and client tricks made them invisible before), pygmy `tiny`. Unseen in the game. Toughness (`tough`): durability climbs the ladder (Armen: "diamond mobs are really strong, so you need a diamond sword"): iron and gold mobs shrug off 1 of every hit, diamond 3, netherite 4 (`shrugOff` in `twists.js` heals it back), so fists do nothing to an iron mob and a stone sword does 2 to a diamond one; a big enough hit still kills. Side materials are plain for now.
- Twists (`twists.js`): per-material `tick` (every second, for loaded painted mobs), `hurt`, `hit` and `die`. Slime hops and ignores fall damage. Honey is slow and slows whoever hits it. TNT dies with a harmless bang that flings what it leaves. A TNT creeper is now a normal creeper whose crater is sand and TNT blocks (unlit). More themed wands are proposed in `docs/wand-variety-proposal.md`.
- Each material's `drop` is its tier's common material, and `rare` holds its rare drops: an item (10%) or an `[item, chance]` list. These are for death drops now and production in phase 2.
- The Iron Wand: a golem keeps the vanilla iron golem sheet, an Iron Wand on a painted golem restores a real `minecraft:iron_golem`, and an all-iron head golem is stored as `iron_painted`, because the head golem's `iron` value predates the wand and means an iron body with the head in its own colors.
- Painted mobs use one sheet per material, so climate looks, wolf collars, angry eyes and sheep wool colors don't show. Reset gives sheep, foxes and wolves a default look, not the one they had.
- `golemgun:head_golem` is one mob: its `golemgun:head` property picks a model from a render controller array, and `head` plus `material` pick a texture sheet. Head sheets stack the golem's 128x128 sheet over the head mob's sheet.
- `setProperty` only lands on the next tick in the real game, and `getProperty` reads the old value until then. The mock applies it at once, so a same-tick read passes there and fails in the game.
- To add a material or a mob, append to `MATERIALS` or `MOBS` in `make_pack.py`. Never reorder them: worlds store the values. `mat` holds 256 materials; each adds ~150 KB of mob sheets (and ~360 KB more if it ever gets head golem sheets, which only `LEGACY` has). Block tiles that are animated strips (magma) or have see-through edges (cake) are cropped before painting.
- Some mob sheets use alpha as a dye mask, not as transparency: the sheep's bare skin has alpha 0 with real colour under it. `has_paint()` in `make_pack.py` handles this, so check a new mob's sheet for holes. Mark such mobs `alpha_mask` so their head golem sheet is drawn solid. Spider and enderman eyes glow through low alpha; `glow` leaves those pixels unpainted.
- Some sheets are `.tga`, not `.png` (drowned, spiders, enderman), and they fill unused areas with junk the game never draws.
- Old models inherit with `geometry.a:geometry.b` (the witch's head is the villager's); `vanilla_geometries` resolves that. Head golems take the head bone and everything under it, except where `head_bones` names bones (the enderman's face is its `hat`).
- The game's own entity files store filter fields as numbers (`"subject": 1`, `"operator": 0`, `"AND"`). `text_filters()` rewrites them as `"other"`, `"=="`, `all_of`, and stops on a number it can't name. Golems made from the number form seemed peaceful to Armen in 0.3.2.
- Vanilla ships as stacked packs (`vanilla`, `vanilla_1.14`, ... `vanilla_1.26.60`), and later layers replace files. Reading only the base `vanilla` pack gives definitions years out of date. `make_pack.py` handles this.

## Changing things

Bump the version in both manifests, their names and dependencies, and `VERSION` in `main.js`. `build.sh` fails if they disagree. Then `./build.sh` and `tools/install.sh`.

## Debug tools

The Golem Debug item opens a menu, and holding it shows what the mob you look at is made of (tier, common and rare material). `/scriptevent golem:give|lineup|reset|despawn|tp|status`, `golem:spawn <mob>` for any mob in `MOBS` (anything else spawns an iron golem), `golem:give_wand <material>` (wand materials only), `golem:set_element <material>` (any material, hybrids too; the mob you look at, else the nearest converted one), and `golem:speed <n>` (a world dynamic property; production reads it once it exists). The lineup includes monsters, which attack.
